About Bryce

I am currently a junior at West Virginia University, where my passion for sports and storytelling collide. As the Sports Managing Editor for The Daily Athenaeum, I oversee a team of writers and photographers, ensuring we capture the pulse of campus athletics. My time at Jomboy Media has sharpened my production skills, teaching me the nuances of digital engagement and viral content creation. Whether I'm behind the camera, in the editing suite, or writing a feature story, my goal remains the same: to tell stories that matter, in a way that people can't stop watching.
